## Key Findings
- **Total roster: 127 heroes.** Attribute split (Liquipedia/official, corroborated by winio.ai July 2026): **Strength 36, Agility 35, Intelligence 34, Universal 22 = 127.** Newest hero: Largo (bard/frog).
- **Facets gone.** GosuGamers (25 March 2026): Valve's 7.41 update "outright removes the 'Facet' mechanic," dropped mid-ESL One Birmingham 2026 (client release March 24). Facets had existed since Patch 7.36 (May 22, 2024). Older tier lists/guides that still discuss facets predate this.
- **Meta shape (7.41e, patch released July 31, 2026):** durable strength frontliners and late-scaling cores dominate. Consensus S-tier by role: **Carry** — Spectre (highest carry win rate at 55.5% in higher brackets per Immortalboost), Lifestealer, Phantom Lancer, Faceless Void (the most-picked carry of the patch at ~15.9% pick rate, Legend+, back on Chronosphere by default), Juggernaut; **Mid** — Invoker (top mid at 15.9% pick / 53.6% win, boosted by the Facet removal handing him extra skill points in 7.41b), Storm Spirit, Ember Spirit, Huskar; **Offlane** — Axe, Timbersaw, Mars, Night Stalker; **Support** — Bounty Hunter, Spirit Breaker, Earthshaker, Jakiro, Shadow Shaman, Lich, Oracle, Bane.

## Caveats
- **Coverage is partial by design.** This deliverable gives you authoritative roster/patch corrections plus ~30 fully sourced hero builds spanning all five roles — not individually verified timing data for all 127 heroes. That full set was not achievable within the research budget, and fabricating timings for the other ~90 heroes would have violated your own "no invented minute-by-minute data" rule. The reproducible method to finish them is above.
- **D2PT samples** are 7000–8500 MMR pub + pro matches over a rolling 14-day window, so exact averages drift day to day and can differ by a minute or two from what you see live; the (Ym–Zm) ranges already capture most of that variance.

- **Tier-list positioning** cited from esports.gg, playhub, Immortalboost, boostingmarket, TeamSmurf and rankedmeta reflects the 7.41d/7.41e window and community/boosting-site opinion, not Valve data — treat as directional.
